OUR STORY

Ethyra Consulting Services is a research-driven social impact consulting firm committed to building responsible, inclusive, and measurable social impact. We collaborate with corporates, NGOs, and institutions to design ethical, community-centered projects backed by evidence and practical insights. Our work combines structured frameworks, field understanding, and transparent processes to create solutions that genuinely benefit people and the planet.
