03. WHY ETHYRA?

Good work needs good thinking.

Social impact work operates in complex environments. Community needs evolve, implementation presents unexpected challenges, and outcomes are rarely explained by a single factor.

Our approach combines a foundation in social work, research-led practice, and a commitment to making evidence useful.

01

Context before conclusions.

We seek to understand the problem, the people affected, and the circumstances surrounding it before recommending a response.

02

Evidence before claims.

We value transparent methods, credible findings, and honest interpretation over unsupported impact statements.

03

People beyond numbers.

We recognise the value of community perspectives alongside quantitative information in understanding change.

04

Learning beyond reporting.

We aim to turn findings into recommendations that support better programmes and more informed decisions.

04. OUR APPROACH

The 4E Framework

Explore. Establish. Evaluate. Evolve.

Our 4E Framework reflects how we connect understanding, planning, evaluation, and learning across the programme lifecycle.

The four stages are connected rather than strictly linear. New evidence may reveal a different need, challenge an earlier assumption, or call for a revised programme design.

The framework provides structure while allowing each engagement to respond to the realities of its context.
