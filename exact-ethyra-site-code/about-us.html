The 4E Framework

Explore. Establish. Evaluate. Evolve.

Our 4E Framework reflects how we connect understanding, planning, evaluation, and learning across the programme lifecycle.

The four stages are connected rather than strictly linear. New evidence may reveal a different need, challenge an earlier assumption, or call for a revised programme design.

The framework provides structure while allowing each engagement to respond to the realities of its context.

WE ARE INCUBATED AT THE MADRAS SCHOOL OF SOCIAL WORK

We are proud to be incubated at the Madras School of Social Work (MSSW), a leading institution in social sciences and development practice.

People-centred by training.
Evidence-led by practice.

Founded by MSW graduates of the Madras School of Social Work, Ethyra brings together a foundation in social work, research-led practice, practical field understanding, and guidance from an experienced advisory team.

We combine people-centred thinking with structured research and evaluation to help organisations approach social development challenges with greater clarity and rigour.

For us, evidence is not just something to report at the end. It is a way to ask better questions, improve decisions, and strengthen the work along the way.

Our approach brings together three essential elements.

People and context
Understanding community priorities, stakeholder perspectives, and the circumstances surrounding a social challenge.

OUR PRINCIPLES

The standards behind our work.

We remain committed to delivering responsible, transparent, and measurable social impact. Every engagement is guided by ethical practice, community realities, and a deep responsibility toward creating sustainable and meaningful change.

01 · Integrity
01 / OUR PRINCIPLES

Integrity in interpretation

We aim to represent findings faithfully, communicate uncertainty, and avoid conclusions that extend beyond the available evidence.

  • Faithfully representing data, community realities, and field findings without distortion.
  • Proactively communicating uncertainty, nuances, and evidentiary boundaries.
  • Resisting unsupported claims that reach beyond credible empirical evidence.
CORE STANDARD Faithful Representation
VALUE DELIVERED Defensible, High-Integrity Evidence
02 · Respect
02 / OUR PRINCIPLES

Respect for people

We value dignity, informed participation, confidentiality, and responsible handling of the information people share.

  • Upholding participant dignity, agency, and lived community experiences.
  • Ensuring voluntary, informed participation supported by transparent consent.
  • Protecting respondent confidentiality and safeguarding shared data responsibly.
CORE STANDARD Dignity & Privacy
VALUE DELIVERED Deep Community Trust & Safety
03 · Rigour
03 / OUR PRINCIPLES

Rigour in method

We select approaches that fit the research question, available evidence, and context rather than applying the same methodology to every engagement.

  • Customizing research design to fit the exact inquiry and field context.
  • Integrating qualitative understanding with rigorous quantitative measurement.
  • Rejecting rigid, one-size-fits-all templates in favor of structured precision.
CORE STANDARD Fit-for-Purpose Method
VALUE DELIVERED Methodological Precision
04 · Transparency
04 / OUR PRINCIPLES

Transparency in limitations

We recognise that evidence has boundaries. We make assumptions, data gaps, and methodological limitations visible wherever they affect interpretation.

  • Explicitly detailing assumptions, sampling constraints, and analytical gaps.
  • Clearly outlining the boundaries of what the evidence can and cannot prove.
  • Equipping decision-makers with risk-aware, nuanced, and honest interpretation.
CORE STANDARD Visible Boundaries
VALUE DELIVERED Honest & Risk-Aware Strategy
05 · Learning
05 / OUR PRINCIPLES

Commitment to learning

We treat findings as opportunities for reflection and improvement, not simply as conclusions to be documented.

  • Treating evaluative findings as catalysts for practical programme refinement.
  • Fostering reflective feedback loops that strengthen subsequent programme cycles.
  • Transforming findings into lasting organizational capacity and sustained impact.
CORE STANDARD Reflective Action
VALUE DELIVERED Continuous Programme Evolution
