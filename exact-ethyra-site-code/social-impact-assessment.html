We deliver rigorous, data-driven Social Impact Assessments that help organizations understand what truly changed, why it changed, and how it can be strengthened or scaled.

Our assessments are independent, methodologically sound, and aligned with global evaluation standards — thoughtfully contextualized for the Indian CSR ecosystem.

Why Social Impact Assessment?

Social Impact Assessment goes beyond activity reporting. It examines meaningful behavioural, social, and economic change in people’s lives — not just outputs delivered.

A well-executed SIA:

Captures real transformation through structured, indicator-based evaluation

Ensures community voices inform findings and future direction

Enables data-backed strategic decision-making

Strengthens transparency for boards, auditors, donors, and regulators

Builds credible, evidence-based impact narratives

Identifies scalable, cost-effective, and sustainable models

SIA transforms data into institutional confidence.

Our SIA Methodology

We follow a refined mixed-method evaluation framework that ensures credibility, analytical depth, and actionable intelligence.

Project Understanding & Indicator Architecture

We begin by understanding the project’s intent, theory of change, and implementation landscape. Clear, outcome-oriented indicators are established to ensure purposeful and aligned measurement.

Mixed-Method Data Collection

We combine quantitative tools and qualitative engagement — including surveys, interviews, focus discussions, and field observations — to capture both measurable results and lived experiences.

Analytical Review & Interpretation

Data is systematically analysed using statistical and thematic methods to identify patterns of change, performance drivers, and areas for improvement.

Independent & Ethical Evaluation

We apply an objective, structured lens guided by globally respected evaluation parameters: relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, impact, and sustainability. Our independence ensures transparency, credibility, and professional integrity.

Strategic Impact Reporting

Findings are presented through concise dashboards, executive summaries, and actionable recommendations designed to inform leadership decisions and future program design.
