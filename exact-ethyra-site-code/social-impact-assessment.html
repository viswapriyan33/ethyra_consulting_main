03 // Social Impact Assessment

Social Impact Assessment — Go beyond the numbers. Understand the significance of change.

Programme data can show what happened. Understanding the significance of those changes requires a closer look at outcomes, stakeholder experiences, context, and the factors that may have contributed.

We conduct research and assessment to help organisations develop a more credible understanding of programme outcomes and their implications for communities.

What we do

Social Impact Assessments
Beneficiary and Stakeholder Research
Qualitative and Quantitative Research
Outcome Analysis
Contribution Analysis
Social Return on Investment (SROI), where appropriate
Impact Reporting and Evidence-Based Recommendations

What you gain

A clearer picture of the outcomes observed, the experiences of people affected, the intervention's plausible contribution, and the lessons that can inform future decisions.

Best suited for

Organisations seeking to assess programme outcomes, understand social value, strengthen impact reporting, or inform decisions about future investment.
